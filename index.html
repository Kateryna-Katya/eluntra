Valley of Dreams

A Place Between Dreams and Reality

Time gets weird in the Valley of Dreams.
Lantern light reflects on water, wooden temples stand quietly among blossoms, and every sound feels intentional.

We wanted to make something that actually chills you out. None of that seizure-inducing flash nonsense or constant ping-ping-ping notifications. Just something that pulls you in naturally.

The Gods Who Guide the Journey

Ebisu, Daikokuten, Bishamonten, Benzaiten, Jurōjin, Hotei, and Fukurokuju are not rulers here — they are guardians.

Each god represents a different aspect
of fortune:
prosperity, wisdom, protection, joy, longevity, harmony. You'll spot them here and there as you play, helping clear the way for better results.

The Game Experience

The game unfolds on a clear, balanced field framed by traditional Japanese motifs.
Symbols move with purpose, and progress feels like a natural continuation rather than a sudden jump.
The way it plays just feels right - like each move sets up the next one without you having to think too hard about it.

DREAM MECHANICS

  • Symbol Removal Flow

    When certain symbol combinations appear, those symbols are gently removed from the field. This creates more space and clarity as the round continues. A dedicated Symbol Removal Bar visually tracks which elements have faded away, helping players understand how the field is evolving.

  • Multiplier Rhythm

    Each successful drop lights up a segment of the multiplier bar. When five segments are active, the multiplier rises. If the flow pauses and no new combinations appear, the light slowly fades and the multiplier returns to its calm state. It's got this organic feel to it - sometimes things get intense, sometimes they're super chill. Kind of like how you breathe without thinking about it.

  • Extended Dream Phase

    During special dream sequences, the multiplier continues to grow as long as the flow remains uninterrupted.
    Once the movement stops, the energy settles and the multiplier becomes
    inactive, returning the experience to
    stillness.
